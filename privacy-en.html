Language Repeater Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Developer: wangzixu (individual developer)

Contact email: wangzixu0612@gmail.com

This policy explains how Language Repeater (Chinese name: 复读机; Android package: com.language.repeater) handles your information. The app provides local audio and video playback, repetition, subtitle display, and audio sentence segmentation. It currently does not offer account registration or sign-in.

1. Media and local data

With your permission, the app reads audio and video files and related information on your device to display the media library and play media. Subtitle files you select are also processed on your device.

Playback and sentence segmentation process media locally. These features do not intentionally upload your audio, video, or subtitle files to the developer or Firebase. Media item information, playback history, playback positions, preferences, sentence segmentation data, and audio processing caches are stored on your device.

The app requests media read permissions for these features. Older Android versions may use storage permissions to handle media files. The app also uses a foreground service for background playback and network connections to send the analytics and diagnostic data described below.

2. Analytics and crash reporting

The app uses the following Google services. They may automatically collect data after the app starts and send it to Google over the network.

Google Analytics for Firebase

This service helps us understand how many people use the app, how often it is used, and usage patterns. It may collect app instance identifiers, advertising identifiers (depending on your device and configuration), device and app information, usage events such as app launches and sessions, and approximate location derived from network information such as IP addresses. The app does not request precise device location permission.

Firebase Crashlytics

This service helps us identify and fix crashes. It may collect installation and session identifiers, device model, operating system version, app version, crash time, exception details, stack traces, and other diagnostic information. Exception details may include technical information such as file paths involved in an error.

These services do not require you to create an app account. The app currently does not provide an in-app switch to disable analytics or crash reporting.

For information about how Google handles data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security

3. Data recipients and security

The analytics and diagnostic data described above is processed through services provided by Google and may be processed on servers outside your country or region. The developer can view analytics reports and crash diagnostics through the relevant consoles.

Analytics and diagnostic data is sent using encrypted connections. We do not sell your personal information. No storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

We may disclose relevant information when required by applicable laws or valid judicial or regulatory requests.

4. Data retention and deletion

Data on your device

Local data is retained until you clear the app storage, uninstall the app, or the app removes the relevant data. The “Clear cache” setting only removes some processing caches; it does not delete all playback history or preferences. Caches for media currently in use may be retained.

You can revoke media access permissions or clear this app’s storage in Android system settings. Revoking media permissions may affect the media library and playback, but does not by itself disable Firebase analytics. Clearing app storage does not delete original audio or video files stored outside the app-specific storage area.

System backups

Your operating system may back up app data according to your device settings. Such backups are managed by the system backup service. Clearing local data does not guarantee deletion of existing cloud backups. You can manage backups through the relevant backup service settings.

Analytics and diagnostic data

Google Analytics event-level data retention is set to 2 months, and user-level data retention is set to 14 months. These settings primarily apply to the corresponding user-level and event-level detailed data. Aggregated reports may be retained longer. Processing is also subject to Google’s applicable rules.

According to Firebase documentation, Crashlytics generally retains crash stack traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning their removal from live and backup systems.

Clearing local data or uninstalling the app does not guarantee immediate deletion of analytics or diagnostic data already sent. To ask questions or request access to, correction of, or deletion of data relating to you, contact us at the email address above. We handle requests according to applicable law, the service provider’s capabilities, and the extent to which the relevant data can be identified. You do not need to send audio or video files by email.

5. Children and minors

We do not intend to collect children’s personal information. If you are a minor, use the app with guidance from your parent or guardian. If a parent or guardian believes the app has collected information about a minor that should not have been collected, please contact us. We will review the matter and respond according to applicable law.

6. This website

This app introduction and privacy policy website does not include Firebase Analytics, advertising, or other active analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process access logs, including IP addresses and request information, to provide and protect its services. The site currently uses GitHub Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

7. Policy updates and contact

When app features or data handling practices change, we will update this policy and the date at the top of this page. We will provide notice or obtain consent where required by applicable law.

For privacy questions, contact wangzixu, the individual developer, at wangzixu0612@gmail.com.
